# Equal ownership and proposed decision policy

OpenForge AI is hosted by the Light-Dataforge organization. lighthuska-lab,
ryanbranson-lab and robertcurran-lab each have verified active owner membership
and equal administrative access. No permanent leader is designated. API owner
roles are reported as admin; they are not module leadership assignments.

The initial implementation has not received independent review. Common account
control, administrative roles and published files are not evidence of independent
collaboration or approval. No reviews or coauthorship are assumed.

Proposed decision policy: record alternatives and dissent in ADR PRs; important
architecture/governance changes require explicit agreement of all three owners.
Routine PRs require one genuine independent reviewer; authors cannot approve their
own work. Temporary module maintainers rotate by agreement, without superior
project authority. Disputes pause the contested change. Releases and ownership,
visibility, security or paid-resource changes require explicit user approval.
Never switch accounts to manufacture approvals or coauthorship.
